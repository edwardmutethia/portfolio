Project overview

A public information service with an operational backbone.

JITUME TVET is designed as more than a brochure website. It combines a public programme and opportunity directory, a versioned JSON API, encrypted citizen and stakeholder intake, and a permission-gated administration portal.

The public side is server-rendered for clear content discovery and search. The internal side supports publishing, review, media, submissions, users, roles, audit history, and site settings. Queue workers and scheduled jobs handle notifications, retention, publication expiry, and media cleanup.

The problem

Opportunity information is fragmented and difficult to verify.

People looking for digital-skills pathways can encounter outdated programme details, informal sources, and unclear next steps. Institutions and partners also need a dependable way to publish approved information without editing an unstructured collection of pages.

The product problem is therefore both public and operational: give residents a trustworthy discovery experience while giving programme teams a controlled way to manage the underlying content.

The users

Different workflows, one source of published truth.

  • Learners and job seekers need current programmes, eligibility guidance, resources, and legitimate next steps.
  • TVET institutions and trainers need structured programme, opportunity, and resource workflows.
  • Partners and sponsors need accurate public visibility and a controlled engagement path.
  • Internal staff need review, approval, publishing, submission handling, roles, and auditability.

The solution

One platform across discovery, intake, and administration.

Public experience

Programme catalogues, opportunity pages, stories, news, events, partners, resources, FAQs, downloads, search, and accessibility/support pages are backed by a shared content model and publication state.

Directory API

A versioned /api/v1 surface exposes published programme, opportunity, county, story, news, event, resource, FAQ, search, and submission-intake endpoints without exposing arbitrary draft records.

Secure intake and administration

Citizen and stakeholder submissions include validation, consent capture, rate limits, idempotency keys, encrypted sensitive fields, retention dates, and queued notifications. Administration uses role and permission checks for every protected surface.

Engineering

Operational concerns designed into the architecture.

Application and data

The active application uses Laravel, Blade, Vite, PostgreSQL, and Redis. Models cover content lifecycle, users, permissions, submissions, media, audit records, settings, and publication state. Database migrations and seed data are separated from normal application startup.

Publishing integrity

Ordinary content changes use a four-eyes review flow with exact-revision checks, publication and expiry states, and immutable audit events. Public content is served only after publication rules are satisfied.

Infrastructure

Docker Compose defines Nginx, PHP-FPM, the application, a queue worker, a scheduler, PostgreSQL, and Redis. Internal networking keeps data services private, while health checks, persistent volumes, backup/restore scripts, and retry-aware queues support operational readiness.

Quality and security

The repository includes PHPUnit unit and feature coverage, API and authorization tests, upload security tests, Playwright checks at mobile widths, Axe accessibility checks, dependency audits, and container builds. No current test count is claimed here without rerunning the checkout.

Challenges & decisions

Protect trust without making the public product difficult to use.

Public discovery and internal governance have different security and consistency requirements. The solution keeps the public experience simple while moving sensitive operations behind explicit authorization, validation, and workflow boundaries.

  • Published directory records are separated from draft and expired content.
  • Sensitive intake fields are encrypted, rate-limited, idempotent, and assigned retention dates.
  • Content publication uses exact revision checks to prevent approving a document that changed after review.
  • Database, Redis, and media services are not exposed as public application ports.
  • Local centre-directory data was retired in favor of a configured external directory boundary.

The key decision was to make operational integrity part of the product model, not an admin-only afterthought.

Results

A code-ready platform with an explicit path to production.

What is evidenced

The repository now contains a complete public portal, versioned directory API, secure intake flows, publishing workflow, administration portal, containerized services, operational scripts, and broad automated test categories.

Production launch still depends on infrastructure choices, secret custody, TLS, mail delivery, monitoring, backup restoration, governance staffing, and representative load testing. Learner, employment, income, adoption, and performance metrics are not claimed because verified outcome data is not available.
